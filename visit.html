Delivery

Free over ₹1000. ₹50 below.

We deliver anywhere you are. The ₹50 fee applies within our local zone. For farther addresses we confirm the fee by distance, in chat, before you pay. Add a little more to your order and the bar in your cart shows when delivery turns free.

Start an order
Do you deliver everywhere?

Yes, there is no distance limit. Delivery is free on orders of ₹1000 or more and ₹50 below that within our local zone. For farther addresses we confirm the fee by distance on WhatsApp or Telegram before you pay.

How do I pay?

UPI or Google Pay to kajirana94@oksbi, or cash on delivery. The order summary includes a payment link.

What are your hours?

Every day from 7 am to 11 pm.

Can I reserve a table?

Message us on WhatsApp with your date, time and group size. We seat guests on the ground floor and upstairs by the river.

Can I order when you are closed?

Yes. Choose "Schedule for later" at checkout and pick a time. We confirm your pre-order as soon as we open at 7 am.

Is there vegetarian food?

Yes. Spinach Momos, Veg Thai Curry, Banana Pancakes, the Veggie Feast and more are vegetarian, and every dish page shows allergens.
